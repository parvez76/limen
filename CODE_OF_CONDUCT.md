# Code of conduct

Parvez follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, critique code and ideas rather than people, and welcome newcomers. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability) and say it's a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
